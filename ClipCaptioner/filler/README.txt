Put your own filler footage here - Minecraft parkour, Subway Surfers, whatever
you want running under the talking head.

Any .mp4 in this folder is used ahead of anything fetched from Pexels, which
has no game footage at all: asked for "minecraft parkour" it returns people
jumping between real rooftops.

Portrait or landscape both work; it is cropped to fill its strip either way.
More than one file means one is chosen at random per clip, so a batch does not
show the same footage under every video.
